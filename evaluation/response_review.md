# Response Quality Review

Review date: 2026-10-07
Method: AI-assisted qualitative review of saved responses.
No new model requests were made.

## Sample

Baseline cases:
P01, P04, N01, N03, N04, Q01, Q03, Q04, Q05, Q06, Q07.

Challenge cases:
C06, M01-2.

Total: 13 responses.

## Findings

- Both sampled positive-feedback responses were warm and relevant.
- All four sampled complaint responses acknowledged the issue
  and used an appropriate apology.
- Status responses clearly identified the ticket and its status.
- Clarification responses explained the required customer input.
- No sampled response promised a refund, a resolution deadline,
  or staff contact.
- The foreign-ticket response did not disclose another customer's data.
- The unsupported banking question received an explanation of scope.

## Limitations

This was an AI-assisted review, not an independent human evaluation.
The sample was selected from existing evaluation reports.
No numerical empathy score or general quality guarantee is claimed.
Automated functional pass rates are reported separately.

## Optional improvement

Use a natural greeting before personalized feedback acknowledgements.
